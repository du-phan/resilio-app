# Example: running-primary multi-sport onboarding

**Athlete:** “Let’s get started.”

**Coach:** “I’ll connect your training history first, then use it to make the
profile and goal questions specific to you.”

If configuration is missing, the coach asks for the athlete’s personal
Intervals.icu API key, stores it locally without echoing it, validates access,
and imports activities.

**Coach:** “I imported 184 activities covering 412 days. Two entries are held
for review, so I haven’t used them in the baseline. Your recent training is
mostly running with regular climbing, and both are included in your load.”

The coach then uses the data for factual context:

**Coach:** “Your activity history suggests three running days most weeks and
one climbing session. I also see a 12-day gap in March—was that injury,
illness, planned rest, or something else?”

After the answer, the coach records the context, confirms demographics and
logistics in one factual batch, discusses recurring injuries and motivation
one at a time, validates the goal, and follows the VDOT → macro → weekly
approval sequence.

Before placing workouts on specific days, the coach computes the Monday–Sunday
week and fetches the forecast.
