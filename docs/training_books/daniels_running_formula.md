# Daniels' Running Formula — source-only methodology record

## Bibliography

- Jack Daniels, *Daniels' Running Formula*, Human Kinetics.
- Resilio's current conceptual reference is the fourth edition. Page/table
  references and the edition's training-pace tables are not available in a
  verified local source.
- The separate race-performance calculator implements the published
  Daniels–Gilbert oxygen-demand and sustainable-fraction equations; it does
  not reproduce the book's training-pace tables.

## Conceptual scope

The methodology organizes training around current performance capacity,
purpose-specific sessions, controlled intensity, recovery, and periodization.
VDOT is a performance-equivalence construct, not a direct measurement of
laboratory oxygen uptake and not permission to infer arbitrary training paces.

## Resilio boundary

This file is descriptive and has no operational authority. Until an
edition-specific pace source is verified and encoded as a separately reviewed
policy, Resilio does not manufacture Easy, Marathon, Threshold, Interval, or
Repetition paces from VDOT. Exact workouts must use athlete-approved RPE or
provider-native heart-rate guidance; pace targets remain absent unless another
verified evidence source supplies them.

No volume progression, workout constructor, recovery rule, or pace lookup in
prose is executable.
