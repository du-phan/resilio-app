# Faster Road Racing — source-only supporting record

## Bibliography

- Pete Pfitzinger and Philip Latter, *Faster Road Racing: 5K to Half
  Marathon*, Human Kinetics.
- Edition, publication year, ISBN, and page/table references have not been
  verified from a licensed local copy.

## Conceptual scope

This supporting source discusses periodized preparation for road races from
5K through the half marathon, including endurance, threshold, race-specific,
speed, taper, and recovery concepts.

## Resilio boundary

This record cannot replace the plan's selected primary methodology and cannot
create numeric prescriptions. It intentionally omits pace tables, volume
progressions, cross-sport duration conversions, and workout templates.
Athlete-confirmed macro fields and executable planning policy are the only
operational plan authority.
