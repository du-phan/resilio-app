# Advanced Marathoning — source-only methodology record

## Bibliography

- Pete Pfitzinger and Scott Douglas, *Advanced Marathoning*, Human Kinetics.
- Edition, publication year, ISBN, and page/table references must be verified
  from the exact edition before any numeric prescription is encoded.

## Conceptual scope

The work describes marathon-specific periodization, progressive endurance
development, long runs, lactate-threshold work, race-specific preparation,
tapering, and recovery. These concepts may explain the rationale of an
athlete-approved marathon plan.

## Resilio boundary

This file is not a plan generator. It contains no executable volume
progression, long-run percentage, pace zone, taper, recovery, or cross-training
conversion. The controlled methodology registry binds a plan to the exact
bytes of this record; the macro skeleton and athlete-confirmed constraints own
all numeric choices.

Intervals.icu native load, fitness, fatigue, zones, and wellness are execution
evidence. They do not by themselves select or modify a Pfitzinger plan.
