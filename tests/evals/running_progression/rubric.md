# Forward coaching evaluation rubric

Two independent agents receive only the scenarios and current coaching
instructions. They do not receive these expectations or prior coaching answers.
Review observable decisions, calculations, qualifications, and workflow routing;
no keyword matching or mandatory exact alternative distance.

For each pass, verify:

- Low-volume extension separates 56.5% share from about 27.4% recorded distance
  extension, handles incomplete coverage, and avoids comparing 100 nominal
  minutes as a physiological percentage to moving/elapsed time.
- Recovery comparison uses completed 15 km still within 30 days (13.3% to 17 km),
  not the 9.5 km cutback; assesses supporting volume and conditions.
- Missed 13/15 km targets never become completion evidence; recommends a
  concrete revised progression without cramming missed training.
- Persistent pain after undercompletion does not justify escalation. Routes to
  reassessment and appropriate professional evaluation without diagnosing.
- Equal shares do not equate the absolute loads or supporting frequency.
- Barcelona no-climbing report stays scoped to its dates. Easy HR is qualified
  effort evidence, not tissue-capacity proof; recovery week stays purposeful.
- Unsupported approved total produces a same-goal macro amendment proposal,
  not filler mileage or automatic closure/new cycle.
- Race-goal answer distinguishes equivalence, endurance preparation and easy
  effort; expresses honest uncertainty without invented probability or paces.
- Reuses already answered recovery questions, provides one recommendation and
  concrete fallback, distinguishes approval from advice, and avoids unsupported
  hard physiological thresholds.

Record failures by scenario ID with the quoted observable output and required
instruction or implementation correction. Re-run affected cases after changes.
